FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is Latticly different from a sequencing tool?

Sequencing tools send the messages you write. Latticly does the thinking — research, copy, replies, scheduling — and only loops in a human when a meeting is ready to book.

Will the outreach sound like a robot?

No. Latticly writes in your voice and references real, specific context about each prospect. Most recipients can't tell it isn't a person on your team.

Which tools does it integrate with?

Major CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot), email providers (Google, Outlook), and calendar tools. Custom integrations are available on Enterprise.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are live within a day. Connect your stack, define your ICP, approve the messaging, and Latticly starts working.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we honor opt-outs and sending limits, and Enterprise includes SSO, SOC 2, and a custom DPA.
